# Pera o Kahon is the one segment, chosen over the other Revillame games

Revillame hosts a lot of segments — Willie of Fortune, Pera o Kahon, Bigyan ng Jacket 'Yan, Hep Hep Hooray, Patalbugan, PayPayLog, Putukan Na!, 1-2-3 Go. We build exactly one, and we picked **Pera o Kahon** because it is not trivia in costume: it is a decision under incomplete information, which is the literal subject of Module 8. Planning is the thing that reduces the risk of that decision, and Bokya is the failure section dramatised.

The rejected alternatives fail on fit, not on quality. Willie of Fortune and 1-2-3 Go are pure question-and-answer, so they would need the module questions swapped in and would lose whatever made the formats worth borrowing. Patalbugan, PayPayLog, Putukan Na! and Cash Salo are dexterity games with no thinking in them, so there is nothing for them to teach.

The direct consequence, and the reason this is recorded: a quiz and a knockout tournament now sit in front of the segment. They are not decoration. They are the part of the show that carries facts, and the finale only pays off because players arrive at it having survived questions about it.
